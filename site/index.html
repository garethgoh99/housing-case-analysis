How this is worked out

  • Exit: both homes are sold in the exit month. Cash back = sale price − loan still owed − agent fee − legal fees − any seller's stamp duty (condo: 16% / 12% / 8% / 4% if sold within 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 years of S&P). Net gain = cash back − everything you paid in. Return on your cash is the annualised IRR of those monthly flows.
  • CPF used for the home must be refunded to your CPF account, with accrued interest, when you sell. It's still your money, so it's counted as cash back here.
  • Equity is the share of the home you own: what you've paid towards the price plus loan principal repaid, plus any price growth you set. Rent builds no equity.
  • Money not coming back is total paid out minus equity gained: rent, loan interest, stamp duty, legal fees and renovation.
  • The exit date can't be earlier than the end of the HDB MOP, so all three cases are compared over the same period.
  • Loan limits follow MAS rules: up to 75% of price, dropping to 55% if the tenure is over 25 years (HDB flats) or 30 years (private), or runs past age 65. The HDB loan is capped at 25 years.
  • Condo progressive payments: you pay the first stages yourself until your share (100% minus the loan %) is covered, then the bank pays the rest stage by stage. Instalments start the month after each disbursement and are recalculated over the remaining tenure.
  • Rates are held flat for the whole period. Bank packages usually reprice after 2–3 years, so use the SORA picker to test higher rates.
  • Stamp duty uses IRAS residential BSD tiers (1% to 6%). Loan checks use MAS limits: HDB instalment up to 30% of income (MSR); total debt up to 55% (TDSR) at a 4% stress rate. Rough guide only, not financial advice.
